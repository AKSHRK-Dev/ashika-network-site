This Acceptable Use Policy (the "Policy") sets out the rules that let every customer use ASHIKA Network (the "Service") safely and fairly. The Policy is an individual provision under Article 2 of the Terms of Service and forms part of the Terms of Service. Please read it together with the Terms of Service.

Article 1 (Scope)

  1. The Policy applies to the use of the servers, runtime environments, subdomains and all other features provided by the Service.
  2. If the Policy and the Terms of Service conflict, the Policy prevails, as provided in Article 2 of the Terms of Service.
  3. Matters not covered by the Policy follow the Terms of Service.

Article 2 (Prohibited uses)

You must not use the Service for the following purposes or in the following ways. Whether something falls under these is decided by the Service.

  • Mining crypto assets (cryptocurrency), or continuously performing similar computation
  • Using the Service mainly as file storage, or storing extremely large amounts of data
  • Operations intended to relay third-party traffic, such as open proxies, public VPNs, or Tor relays and exit nodes
  • Sending spam or bulk email, or running systems that breed them
  • Acts that disrupt third-party systems, such as DDoS attacks, port scanning and unauthorized access, and use as a stepping stone for attacks on third parties
  • Creating, storing or distributing computer viruses or malware, and operating command-and-control (C2) servers
  • Setting up phishing sites, fraud, and similar acts
  • Acts that violate the terms of third-party services, such as cheating in games or abuse with bots
  • Acts that place a heavy load on third-party servers, such as excessive scraping or crawling
  • Reselling, or lending to third parties, without the Service's consent
  • Any other act that violates laws, public order and morals, or Article 9 (Prohibited acts) of the Terms of Service

Article 3 (Prohibited content)

You must not send or store the following on the Service, or make it viewable by third parties.

  • Child pornography or content depicting child abuse
  • Content that infringes third-party copyrights, trademarks or other intellectual property rights (including illegally uploaded files and pirated copies)
  • Content contrary to public order and morals, such as excessively violent or sexual expression
  • Content that defames third parties or damages their reputation or credit
  • Content that discloses the personal information of third parties without their consent
  • Any other content the Service judges inappropriate

Article 4 (Fair use of resources)

  1. The Service's servers share CPU, memory, disk and network among multiple customers. Please use them fairly so as not to hinder others.
  2. If usage stays close to the limits of your plan, the Service may temporarily stop your server without notice.
  3. If usage repeatedly exceeds the limits, the Service may restrict the use of your server.
  4. Temporarily high load, such as at startup or during builds, is fine. If you need constantly high load, please consider a larger plan.

Article 5 (Use of the network)

  1. Acts that place a heavy load on the Service's network or servers, and unauthorized access or attempts at it against networks or systems, are prohibited.
  2. Acts that disrupt the normal operation of the network, such as spoofing IP addresses or intercepting communications, are prohibited.
  3. The Service may restrict certain ports or traffic to keep the network safe.

Article 6 (Violations)

  1. If you violate the Policy, or the Service judges that you have, the Service may stop or delete your servers and suspend or delete your account without notice, under Article 13 of the Terms of Service.
  2. Even when such measures are taken, there are no refunds, no extension of the contract period, and no return of stored data.
  3. If the Service judges a violation to be malicious, it may report it to the police or other authorities and claim damages.
  4. If you disagree with a measure, you may object through the procedure in Article 15 (Objections) of the Terms of Service.

Article 8 (Changes to the Policy)

  1. The Service may change the Policy when it judges it necessary.
  2. The changed Policy takes effect when it is posted on this website.
